About
Method, accuracy & sources

Method. VO₂ at pace from the selected engine, extrapolated to max via %VO₂ reserve ≈ %HR reserve: VO₂max = 3.5 + (VO₂pace − 3.5) ÷ %HRR. Energy cost from Minetti's gradient polynomial, O₂ → energy at 20.9 kJ·L⁻¹.

Reliability. Most accurate from a sustained, steady effort at ≥70% HRR. Easy runs (<60% HRR) get noisy. Grade = total ascent ÷ distance as one average gradient; descents aren't credited.

Surface factor scales movement (net) cost on top of grade: road ×1.00, grass/firm ×1.05, technical ×1.10, soft/mud/sand ×1.35. Road leaves results unchanged.

Uth cross-check (15.3 × HRmax/HRrest) runs high at very low resting HR, so treat it as a loose upper bound.

Sources. ACSM metabolic equations (11th ed.); Minetti et al., J Appl Physiol 2002; Swain & Leutholtz, MSSE 1997/2004; Uth et al., Eur J Appl Physiol 2004; Pinnington & Dawson, J Sci Med Sport 2001.
