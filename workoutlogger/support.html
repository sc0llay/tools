WorkoutLogger Support

WorkoutLogger lets you log workouts by hand and save them to Apple Health, so they show up in the Health and Fitness apps.

My workout didn't save, or some details are missing

WorkoutLogger can only save what you've allowed. Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > WorkoutLogger and turn on all categories. After a save, the app lists anything it skipped.

Why don't I see my notes in Fitness?

Notes are stored with the workout in Apple Health, but the Fitness app doesn't display them. You can see them in WorkoutLogger's History.

How do I delete a workout?

In WorkoutLogger, open History and swipe left on the workout, or open it and tap Delete Workout. You can also delete it in the Health app.

Can I change kilometres or miles?

Yes. Open the Settings tab in WorkoutLogger and choose your distance and energy units.
