WorkoutLogger Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

WorkoutLogger lets you record workouts by hand and save them to Apple Health. It doesn't collect any data: there's no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

Health data

When you log a workout, WorkoutLogger saves what you enter to Apple Health on your device. That covers the workout type, time and duration, plus any distance, heart rate, calories, elevation, swimming details, effort and notes you add. WorkoutLogger reads back only the workouts it saved itself, so it can show your history and let you repeat or delete them. It doesn't read any other health data.

Where your data is stored

Your health data stays in Apple Health, under your control. If you've turned on Health in iCloud, Apple syncs it to your other devices; that's handled by Apple, not by WorkoutLogger. The developer never receives or sees your data.

Sharing

WorkoutLogger never sends your data anywhere and never shares it with third parties. Health data isn't used for advertising, marketing or tracking.

Your choices

You decide what WorkoutLogger can access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > WorkoutLogger, and you can change that at any time. To delete a workout, swipe it away in WorkoutLogger's History or remove it in the Health app. Deleting the app leaves your workouts in Apple Health until you remove them there.

Preferences

Your unit and default workout choices are stored on your device only.

Children

WorkoutLogger doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
